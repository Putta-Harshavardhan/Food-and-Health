10 expert health tips
Science-backed habits for a healthier life
🥤
Drink water firstStart your morning with a glass of water before any food or caffeine. It kick-starts your metabolism and rehydrates your body after sleep.
🌈
Eat the rainbowAim to include at least 5 different colors of fruits and vegetables daily. Each color represents unique phytonutrients and antioxidants your body needs.
🍽️
Mind your plate portionsUse the plate method: half vegetables, one quarter protein, one quarter whole grains. This automatically creates a balanced, calorie-controlled meal.
🕐
Don't skip breakfastA balanced breakfast with protein and fiber helps stabilize blood sugar, reduces mid-morning cravings, and improves concentration and energy levels.
🥦
Add fiber to every mealFiber from whole foods slows digestion, keeps you full longer, feeds good gut bacteria, and helps lower cholesterol. Aim for 25–35g daily.
🧂
Reduce processed foodsProcessed foods often contain excess sodium, sugar, and unhealthy fats. Cook at home with whole ingredients to control what goes into your body.
🥩
Prioritize proteinProtein builds and repairs tissues, supports immune function, and keeps you full. Include a quality protein source (eggs, legumes, fish, meat) at every meal.
😴
Sleep affects eatingPoor sleep increases hunger hormones (ghrelin) and reduces satiety hormones (leptin), leading to overeating. Aim for 7–9 hours of quality sleep per night.
🧘
Practice mindful eatingEat slowly, chew well, and remove distractions. It takes 20 minutes for your brain to register fullness — rushing meals leads to overeating.
📊
Read nutrition labelsCheck serving sizes, total calories, and the ingredient list. The fewer and more recognizable the ingredients, the better the food choice generally is.
